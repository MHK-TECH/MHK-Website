State-of-the-Art AI Infrastructure

How the big giants design, train, and deploy AI systems — and how to apply those patterns at your scale.

Training Infrastructure

Distributed training across GPU clusters (A100/H100), mixed precision, gradient checkpointing, and data parallelism. Companies like Google and Meta train on thousands of GPUs with frameworks like JAX and PyTorch Distributed.

Model Serving at Scale

TensorRT optimization, model quantization (INT8/INT4), batching strategies, and autoscaling. OpenAI and Anthropic serve millions of requests per minute with sub-100ms latency using custom serving infrastructure.

Vector Databases & RAG

Pinecone, Weaviate, Milvus, and pgvector for semantic search. Modern RAG systems use hybrid search (dense + sparse), re-ranking, and contextual compression for production-grade retrieval.

MLOps & LLMOps

MLflow, Weights & Biases, and Kubeflow for experiment tracking. CI/CD for models, A/B testing, shadow deployments, and automated rollback. Monitoring for drift, latency, and cost.

Edge & On-Device AI

ONNX Runtime, TensorFlow Lite, and Core ML for running models on edge devices. Quantization and pruning to fit models on mobile and IoT without cloud dependency.
